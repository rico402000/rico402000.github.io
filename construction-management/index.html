How a project runs

Four stages, from the lease to the final file

You can bring us in as early as lease negotiations, and we can stay with the project right through close-out. Here is how the work moves.

Early involvement and budgeting

We help create your scope of work and responsibilities, and provide preliminary budget estimates before the project is committed to.

Bidding and contractor selection

Once a project is authorized to move forward, we review your construction requirements, place the project out for bid, and work with bidding contractors to obtain quantifiable estimates, which we present to ownership for review.

Contract and compliance

We issue an AIA (American Institute of Architects) contract to the owner's contractor of choice, then monitor that all proper permits and lien waivers are received and that insurance and bonding requirements are met to the owner's standards.

Payment review and close-out

We review all requests for payments, monitor progress against lease-required deadlines, and follow up to gain warranties, drawings and other important documents for safekeeping.
