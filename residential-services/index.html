Common questions

What homeowners ask us first

Something not covered here? Call (330) 353-1136 and we'll answer it straight.

Do you bag the grass clippings?

Yes. Clippings are bagged as part of the mowing visit, so they aren't left sitting on the lawn or blown into the beds and driveway.

How long will your crew be at my house?

For mowing, we're often on and off the property in as little as 10 minutes. We bring the entire team and the equipment the job needs, so the work gets done in one efficient pass rather than dragging across the afternoon.

Do you clear snow from residential driveways?

We do — driveways and walkways, plowed, shoveled and treated for ice. Add it to a seasonal package or book it on its own; our commercial accounts also get 24-hour emergency coverage.

How do I get pricing?

Call (330) 353-1136 or send us a request and we'll arrange a free, no-obligation estimate. Residential packages are very reasonable and fit into most any budget, and because they're all-inclusive you won't be pricing out each individual job.
