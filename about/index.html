Who we are

Doing the work properly, and staying to look after it

Nations Maintenance has been providing quality commercial property maintenance and landscape services in the Akron-Canton area since 1997.

We emphasize quality of work — not quantity of work — and offer personalized service that is unmatched by our competitors. That has been the whole idea from the beginning: take on the properties we can look after well, and look after them personally, season after season.

We are a locally based company, and we are committed to the communities we serve. The crews you see on your property live and work in the same area you do.

Local and accountable

Your account is handled here, not from out of state

Many of the companies working in this region are large operations based out of state, run from satellite offices. We are not one of them. We handle all of our customer accounts personally out of our Canton offices, so if there is a problem, we can address it right away.

Being local also keeps our costs affordable. We spend most of our time serving our customers' property maintenance and landscaping needs, while large companies spend a large portion of their budget on advertising and sales staff. We would rather save our customers money and provide them with better-quality work.

Quality over quantity

What quality of work actually depends on

Three things decide how a property looks in the middle of August and after the first hard freeze: how much work a company takes on, who does it, and what it spends its money on.

Quality of work, not quantity of work

We would rather do a property properly than add another one we cannot look after. That is why customers stay with us for the long term.

Our own crew, never subcontractors

We do all the work ourselves. Because no one else is sent in our place, we can ensure quality control and provide the best service possible.

Local costs, not national ad budgets

Large companies spend a large portion of their budget on advertising and sales staff. We prefer to save our customers money and give them better-quality work instead.

Our philosophy

Once you are a customer, you are with us for the long term

That is how we have always measured the business. Not by how many properties we can list, but by whether the people who hired us are still with us years later — and whether the work still looks like it should.

Veteran owned

Owned and operated by a veteran

Nations Maintenance is a veteran-owned business. It shows in how we run the company day to day: we show up when we say we will, we take responsibility for the result, and we don't consider a job finished until it's done properly.

As a thank-you to those who served, we take 10% off our maintenance services for veterans — landscaping, lawn care, snow and ice removal, parking lot sweeping and property maintenance. Mention it when you call or send a request and show your VA card, DD-214 or Veteran ID, and we'll apply it to your estimate. Construction management and architectural services are quoted separately, and the discount does not apply to them.
