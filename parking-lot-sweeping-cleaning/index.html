Detailing, not just litter pickup

We're much more than litter getters

The lot is the first thing people see when they arrive at your business. A clean sweep gives them a neat, tidy experience the moment they drive onto the property — before anyone reaches the door.

That's why we concentrate on detailing the property. Every corner, loading dock and dumpster corral gets looked at and cleaned, in addition to the main parking area.

What a sweep includes

  • The main parking area, every corner, loading docks and dumpster corrals
  • Cigarette butts and other litter picked up by hand, including hard-to-reach spots
  • All trash removed from landscape beds and lawn areas
  • Gravel buildup left behind by snow removal cleared away
  • Outdoor trash cans emptied and new liners installed with each sweep

Why it pays off

What a regularly swept lot does for your property

Sweeping is about more than appearance. It keeps the surface in better shape and the whole property presentable, season after season.

A neat, clean appearance

A tidy lot helps you convey a neat and clean appearance to every customer who pulls in.

Fewer potholes and cracks

Regular sweeping helps prevent the potholes and cracks that mechanical weathering causes.

Beds and lawns stay trash-free

All trash is removed from your landscape beds and lawn areas, not just from the asphalt.

Gravel buildup removed

Sweeping clears the gravel buildup left behind from snow removal work over the winter.

Trash cans serviced every visit

Outdoor trash cans are emptied and new liners installed with each sweep — never left overflowing.

Detail in the corners

Cigarette butts and other litter are picked up by hand, along with hard-to-reach corners, loading docks and dumpster corrals.
