Turn-key management, from lease to lien waiver

Our project manager has 25 years of retail construction experience as a commercial property owner representative — and knows what to do when problems come up mid-build.

Local, hands-on, and built to stay that way

We handle every customer account personally out of our Canton offices. If a problem comes up, we address it right away — you're not routed through a call center, and you're not one of hundreds of accounts at an out-of-state company's satellite branch.
