Common questions

What commercial clients ask us first

How fast will someone be on site in an emergency?

We are on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, holidays included. If an issue or concern needs to be handled immediately, we will be on site within two hours to get it done.

Do you use subcontractors for commercial work?

No. Our highly qualified staff handles all of our commercial jobs personally, and we do not use subcontractors — even in an emergency. The same crew is on your property season after season, so nothing about it gets relearned on your time.

How are your maintenance packages priced?

Packages are priced for your property and the services you choose, so it starts with a free, no-obligation estimate. Call (330) 353-1136 or send us a request and we will follow up to arrange a walkthrough.

What is included in an all-inclusive package?

Everything outside: painting, exterior lighting and asphalt repairs, complete parking lot sweeping, snow removal and landscaping. It also covers the repairs we discover while tending to other needs on your property, so you are not pricing out each job separately.
